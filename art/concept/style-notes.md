# Flop Siege — style rules

- Build flat storybook silhouettes from bold, rounded shapes that read on a phone.
- Outline foreground assets in warm dark `#2A2420`; keep stroke weight consistent at matching scale.
- Use the palette's flat base colours and one soft shadow tone per material; avoid detailed textures.
- Light every asset with cheerful warm daylight from the upper left.
- Make castle blocks chunky grey-blue stone and warm wooden planks, with red-and-cream flags.
- Give knights round heads, bucket helmets, silver armour, colourful tabards and loose, readable limbs.
- Keep the king short and round, with an oversized gold crown and a red robe edged in white fur.
- Show harmless slapstick through flops, waves, alarm and pouting; never wounds or violent impacts.
- Keep clouds soft and distant hills muted so the lower-left launch and upper-right castle remain clear.
- Use 1080×1920 portrait backgrounds: empty plateau at `(389,1075)` to the right edge, valley floor at `y=1690`, and tiny tents only at `x<108`; keep both ground edges horizontal.
